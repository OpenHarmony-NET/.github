Hello everyone, I'm CeSun(BuBu).
I have an announcement to make: the Avalonia and .NET projects for HarmonyOS have officially stopped updates and been archived.
In fact, there have been no public updates to the project since last July. At that time, Avalonia was in cooperation negotiations with Huawei. Avalonia promised that once the partnership was finalized, they would hire me either full-time or part-time to continue adapting Avalonia for HarmonyOS. For the interests of both myself and Avalonia, aside from compensation matters, I also needed to confirm subsequent technical details and development specifications with Avalonia’s technical lead. This led me to slow down the development of HarmonyOS adaptation for Avalonia, with all progress only pushed to a private closed-source repository.
However, things did not go as planned. The cooperation between the two parties kept getting delayed. It was not until before the Lunar New Year this year that several outsourcing companies contacted me via Bilibili and CNBlog, seeking cooperation on Avalonia’s HarmonyOS adaptation. That was when I learned that while Huawei was ostensibly continuing partnership talks with Avalonia, it was secretly using the technical information provided by Avalonia to launch a tender for domestic suppliers on the bidding platform. I take full responsibility for these statements and have supporting evidence. The information shared with me by those outsourcing firms is exactly the data Avalonia provided to Huawei. I’ve been told such practices are quite common domestically, so there’s no need for us to dwell on it too much.
Although those outsourcing companies told me I could name any terms to help them win the bid, I already have a full-time job and have no need to seek other employment or additional compensation. Working on Avalonia has always been just a hobby of mine. I was drawn to Avalonia by its remote work model and great team atmosphere, so I have no desire to take a job with an outsourcing company, nor am I willing to take such risks. I therefore declined all their offers.
Until yesterday, someone in the Avalonia developer QQ group noticed that an outsourcing company was recruiting for an Avalonia Framework Engineer on Boss Zhipin. After confirming with Dong Bin, I learned that the Avalonia HarmonyOS adaptation project had been successfully awarded to this outsourcing company. After a string of setbacks, this outcome was largely expected.
Accordingly, all projects under the OpenHarmony-NET organization will cease all updates starting today, and none of the completed development work will be made public. There is little point in open-sourcing it anyway; the outsourcing company will likely release the code publicly later under the name of some SIG organization. Given that the Chinese government offers subsidies for open-source projects, they will almost certainly choose to open-source it eventually.
Lastly, I have no regrets about how everything unfolded, nor do I need netizens to stand up for me. I never relied on this project for income (though additional compensation would have been welcome). I ask all enterprises that use or reference our code to abide by the MIT License, include our project in your license documentation, and acknowledge our contributions.
